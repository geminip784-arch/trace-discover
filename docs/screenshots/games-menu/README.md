# The two-step games menu

Taken in headless Chromium at 1280 x 626 (the tablet in Chrome), from the real app. Before opening the Games book, the browser was given stars saved by the old one-step menu (Numbers, Colors, Shapes, Animals to homes, Sort by kind, Odd by color, Missing piece level 1) and 3 finished rounds of Missing piece level 1.

| File | What it shows |
|---|---|
| `step1-kinds.png` | Step 1: a 200 px tile per kind of game: Match, Follow the path, Sort it, Odd one out, Missing piece. Each has tiny gold stars for its finished sets (Match 3, the others 1). These are the stars from the old menu. |
| `step2-missing.png` | Step 2 for Missing piece: level 1 has its star, level 2 is open (earned), levels 3 and 4 are faint and wiggle when tapped. |
| `step2-match.png` | Step 2 for Match: five 200 px tiles; Numbers, Colors and Shapes keep their stars. |
