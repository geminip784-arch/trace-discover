Find pictures (Slice 5G)

Every picture in the find library, by letter, with its word and the places it belongs in. Each letter's scene uses the place that holds the most of its pictures (the place in bold; a tie is picked at random). Pictures kept out of find scenes are at the end.

Urdu (Slice 10E), after the English letters: each letter's one picture with its Urdu word and its meaning. Its scene is in the picture's place (in bold); the other 6 pictures come from the Urdu pictures, never a look-alike or a letter that shares its sound.
